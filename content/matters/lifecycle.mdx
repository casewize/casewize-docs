# Phase, status and holds

## Phase

Where the litigation actually stands: **Investigation → Discovery → Expert
reports → Mediation → Trial**. Advance or revert it from the overview tab.

Advancing re-evaluates what the client is allowed to see and **releases
nothing** — that stays one deliberate click by the lead attorney. Reverting
never un-shares what was already shared.

A phase change is one of the triggers that starts a fresh analysis run.

<Callout type="info">
  The analysis has a phase vocabulary of its own, finer than this one — intake,
  pre-filing, pleadings, written discovery, depositions, expert discovery,
  mediation, summary judgment, pretrial, trial preparation. It is what the
  engine judges the matter to be in, and it appears on each run.
</Callout>

## Status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | An unfinished intake. Only its creator sees it. |
| **Active** | Live. |
| **On hold** | Paused, and still counted against your plan's active-matter limit. |
| **Closed** | Frozen for every role. |
| **Archived** | Past its retention window. |

## Closing a matter

**Close the matter** on the overview tab. An **outcome** is required — settled,
dismissed, verdict, and so on.

Closing freezes writes for every role and starts the retention clock. Nothing on
a closed matter is editable by anybody: its tasks go read-only, its message
composer disappears while the history stays, and its settings stop saving.

Only the firm owner can reopen it.

## Retention

Retention comes from the firm profile — a number of years after closing, set in
**Firm Settings → Firm**. The clock starts at close, not at open.

A legal hold outranks it: a held matter is never deleted, whatever the retention
setting says.

## Legal hold

A legal hold freezes deletion of the matter and everything under it — documents,
versions, and the audit trail. Setting one requires a typed reason, kept
permanently.

- An attorney or the firm owner can **set** a hold.
- Only the firm owner can **lift** one, with a reason kept forever.
- The hold stops deletion, not editing. Fields still save; nothing is destroyed
  while it is on.

<Callout type="warning">
  Destroying material the firm is required to keep can be treated as destroying
  evidence. That is why lifting a hold sits with one person and is recorded.
</Callout>
