# Case settings

The **Settings** tab edits the matter itself — the same fields the intake
collected, after it went live. Everything saves as one change, written against
the matter.

## Identify the case

**Internal number** — unique within the firm. Changing it renames the file
everywhere it appears.

**Court number** — filled in the day the suit is filed.

**Case title**

## Classify it

Matter type, jurisdiction, court. Your firm's own matter types lead the list.

## What the matter cannot go without

**Statute deadline** and **lead attorney**, both required. The row fails to save
without them, whatever the screen does.

The lead field is read-only here for most people: handing a matter over is done
from the **Team** tab, by the outgoing lead or the firm owner, and it carries
its own decision about whether the outgoing lead stays on the matter.

## When settings will not open

| State | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | A draft is edited in the intake wizard, which owns the conflict check and the blockers. Finish it there. |
| **Closed** | Nothing on a closed matter is editable by any role. Reopening is the firm owner's. |
| **Read-only for you** | You can open the file but not change it. Nothing is hidden — it simply will not save. |
| **Walled / metadata-only** | You administer this matter and cannot read it. |

<Callout type="info">
  A legal hold does not close the settings tab. The hold stops deletion, not
  editing.
</Callout>
