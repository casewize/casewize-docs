# Inside the case file

## The case list

**Cases** shows the matters you are on — and only those. A matter you are
screened off is absent from the table, the counts and the search alike, rather
than hidden inside them.

Columns: case, phase, status, statute deadline, parties, lead, last activity,
and what is waiting on you. Filter by phase across the top. A **Hold** badge
means a legal hold is set and nothing on that matter deletes while it stands.

Above the table, **Needs attention** collects two things:

- a statute deadline inside 30 days (or already passed),
- no activity on the file for 30 days.

## Status and phase at a glance

**Status** is the matter's lifecycle: draft, active, on hold, closed, archived.

**Phase** is where the litigation stands: investigation, discovery, expert
reports, mediation, trial.

Both are explained on [Phase, status and holds](/matters/lifecycle).

## The overview tab

The first thing you see when you open a matter.

**Case strength** — the overall score from the most recent analysis, its
version, and whether it moved. If the matter has never been analysed, this
panel tells you so and offers to start one.

**Open alerts** — what the analysis has raised and nobody has acknowledged.

**Next actions** — recommended actions, most urgent first.

**Material developments** — what the analysis judged material since last time.

**On this matter** — open tasks with overdue and due-this-week counts, the
document count, and the number of chronology events.

Down the side: the statute deadline with days remaining, the lead attorney, the
team size including external grants, the party count, and the conflict-check
result.

## Phase, retention and hold

The overview also carries the controls for advancing the phase, closing the
matter, and setting or lifting a legal hold. See
[Phase, status and holds](/matters/lifecycle).

## Walls you may hit

**"You can see this matter exists. You cannot open the file."** You have a
metadata-only view — the firm-admin position. You can manage the roster; you
cannot read the matter.

**"You administer this matter. You cannot read it."** The same thing, said on
the matter itself. There is no setting that changes it.

**"That matter is not available to you."** You are not on it, and for you it
does not exist.
