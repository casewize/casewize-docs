# What the analysis reads

Every analysis discloses its own coverage. The panel at the top of the Analysis
tab reads, for example, *31 read · 4 withheld*, and opening it accounts for
every document on the matter.

<Callout type="info">
  Coverage is **disclosed, never chosen**. There is no per-run document picker.
  What the engine may read is decided by the flags on each document, and the
  panel tells you what that came to.
</Callout>

## The five buckets

| Bucket | Why | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Read** | Text extracted. | Quotable, and cited in the findings. |
| **Privileged** | The privilege flag. | Never retrieved, never quoted, never produced. |
| **Work product** | The work-product flag. | The firm's own analysis. Never retrieved, never released. |
| **Withdrawn** | Taken off the matter. | The engine no longer opens it. |
| **No file behind it** | Recorded without a document to read. | Off a privilege log, or still uploading. |

## The rule

**Privilege and work product are excluded by the system itself. No role, grant
or setting turns them on.**

That is the entire mechanism. There is nothing to configure, so there is nothing
to misconfigure.

## Making a withheld document readable

Only one thing changes a document's bucket, and it is the document itself:

- Clear the **privileged** or **work product** flag on the document, and it
  becomes eligible on the next run.
- **Restore** a withdrawn document to the matter.
- Upload the file behind a document that was recorded without one.
- Ask for an unreadable document to be [read
  again](/documents/reading#reading-a-document-again).

## When nothing is readable

Two empty states you may hit on the Analysis tab:

**"Nothing has been filed on this matter yet."** File a document.

**"All 6 documents on this matter are withheld from the analysis."** Everything
present is privileged, work product, withdrawn, or has no file. The run refuses
rather than producing an empty analysis.

The same thing said at the moment you press Analyse: *There is nothing on this
matter the analysis may read.*
