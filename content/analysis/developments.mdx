# Developments and alerts

## What changed

The **What changed** section is the analysis's answer to *is this matter
different from last week*. Each development carries why it matters, the new
risks and opportunities it creates, and a recommended response.

Pick the window across the top:

- Since the last analysis
- Since the last upload
- Last 7 days · Last 30 days
- Since the last deposition
- Everything

<Callout type="info">
  *Nothing material in this period* is a real answer, not an empty state. The
  record has grown and nothing in it moved the case — widen the window to see
  earlier developments.
</Callout>

### The fifteen categories

New supporting evidence · New harmful evidence · Contradictory testimony ·
Impeachment material · New witness · Missing evidence · New claim · Weakened
claim · New defense · Increased damages · Reduced damages · Procedural risk ·
Deadline · Settlement leverage · Trial strategy

Each development carries a **materiality**: critical, high, moderate, low or
informational — and a **confidence**: low, medium or high.

## Alerts

Alerts are the subset that wants your attention now. They appear on the matter's
overview, on the Analysis tab, and on the dashboard.

| Kind | Raised when |
| --- | --- |
| Score moved | An overall or category score changed materially. |
| Witness contradicted | The record contradicts a witness. |
| Gap resolved | Something the analysis had asked for arrived. |
| Claim strength changed | |
| Procedural risk | |
| Deadline approaching | |
| Settlement value | |
| Action now urgent | A recommended action's priority rose. |
| Document problem | A document could not be read, or its date is unclear. |
| Impeachment material | |

Each is **New**, **Acknowledged**, **Dismissed** or **Resolved**. Acknowledging
one says you have seen it; dismissing one takes it off the list.

## Choosing what you are told about

**What you are told about**, on the Analysis tab, applies to **this matter
only**:

- **Alert me from** — the minimum severity that raises an alert at all.
- **Show alerts in the app**
- **Email me**
- **Mute this matter**

<Callout type="warning">
  Email carries the case number and the kind of development, never the facts.
  Nothing substantive about a matter is sent to an inbox.
</Callout>

Notifications about tasks and mentions are separate, and configured on
[Your account](/account).

## Case evolution

**Case evolution** lists every version of the analysis with what triggered it —
*after Deposition of R. Alvarez was read*, *started by hand*, *phase changed* —
who started it, whether it was full or incremental, and how it ended.

**Compare two versions** puts any two side by side and reports what moved
between them: developments, gaps opened, gaps closed, actions added.
