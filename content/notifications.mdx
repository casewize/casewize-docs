# Notifications

**Notifications** lists what you have been told, newest first. The bell in the
top bar carries the unread count and **Mark all read** clears it.

## The kinds

| Kind | Raised when |
| --- | --- |
| **Mentions** | Somebody names you in a thread. |
| **Task assignments** | A task is put in your name. |
| **Task due dates** | Once, on the morning a task of yours comes due. |

Analysis alerts are configured **per matter**, not here — see [Developments and
alerts](/analysis/developments#choosing-what-you-are-told-about).

## Email

Notices reach you **in the app either way**. The email settings on [Your
account](/account) decide which of them also reach your inbox, at that firm.

Each firm you belong to has its own setting.

## Withheld notices

<Callout type="info">
  *On a matter you can no longer open.* A notice raised before your access
  changed stays in your list, with its subject removed. The notice is not
  deleted — that would rewrite history — and its contents are not shown.
</Callout>

## What email never carries

Alert email carries the case number and the kind of development, **never the
facts**. Nothing substantive about a matter is sent to an inbox.
