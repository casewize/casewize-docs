# Your account

The only page in the product where you change your own details.

## Profile

**Full legal name** — prints on signature blocks and productions, and names you
in every audit line.

**Job title** — shown to colleagues. Carries no authority of its own.

**State bar number** and **issued by**.

<Callout type="warning">
  The bar number is the important field. With it on file you may approve AI
  output and be made lead on a matter. Clearing it takes both away on your next
  request.

  Approval authority is **derived from the licence you record here**, not
  granted by a checkbox.
</Callout>

**Work email** — your sign-in address. Changing it has to be confirmed from the
mailbox that receives it, which is not wired up yet; ask an administrator.

## Password

Changing your password **signs you out of every other device** immediately.
Minimum eight characters.

## Where you are signed in

Every session, with the device and when it started.

- **Revoke** ends one.
- **Sign out everywhere else** ends all but this one.

Revoking takes effect on the next request from that device, not at its next
sign-in. A session you do not recognise should be revoked.

## Two-factor authentication

Set it up, generate new backup codes, or turn it off — unless your firm requires
it, in which case it cannot be turned off here.

The flow: scan the code with any TOTP app, save the backup codes somewhere that
is **not** the phone you just enrolled, then confirm with a code from the app.
Your current password is asked for first, so that somebody at your unlocked
screen cannot attach their own authenticator to your account.

- A code is not asked for on a trusted device for thirty days.
- Backup codes work once each and are never shown again.
- Generating a new set invalidates the old one immediately.

See [Signing in](/start/signing-in) for the full detail.

## Your role

Shown, and not editable. *A role is granted by an administrator. Being able to
raise your own would make every other permission in the product decorative.*

## Your cases

Three counts, and the distinction between them is the point:

**Matters you can open** — assigned to you and readable.

**Matters you lead** — the only person who may release anything on these.

**Matters you administer** — you know these exist and can manage the roster; you
cannot read the file.

<Callout type="info">
  Ethical walls that apply to you are not listed here. A walled matter shows no
  trace anywhere, and a line saying you are walled off one would be the
  disclosure the wall exists to prevent.
</Callout>

## Email preferences

Which in-app notices also reach your inbox, at this firm: mentions, task
assignments, task due dates. Each firm you belong to has its own setting.

## Appearance

System, light or dark.
