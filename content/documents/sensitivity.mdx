# Privilege and sensitivity

Four flags, two of which change what the system itself will do with the
document.

## Privileged

Withheld from production **and from AI retrieval**. Its text is never retrieved,
never quoted in an analysis, and never produced.

Setting the flag asks for a **basis for the privilege claim** — for example,
*attorney-client, advice sought from counsel on the termination*. That text is
kept with the document.

## Work product

The firm's own analysis. Withheld from AI retrieval, and never reaches an
outside grant.

## Contains PHI

Health information. Every open of the document is logged.

## Contains PII

Personal information about an identifiable person.

## What the flags actually enforce

<Callout type="warning">
  Privilege and work product are **hardcoded exclusions, not permission
  checkboxes**. No role, no grant, no plan and no setting anywhere in the
  product turns them on for the analysis. A firm owner cannot make the engine
  read a privileged document.
</Callout>

Concretely, while either flag stands:

- the document's text is not retrievable, so no stage of an analysis can quote it;
- it cannot be released to the client — the release button refuses;
- it is never included in the document list an external grant can reach;
- it counts under **Withheld** in the analysis coverage panel.

## Changing a flag later

Editing a document to *add* a flag tells you plainly: *this will be withheld
from the AI*. Its text stops being retrievable and stops reaching anyone outside
the firm.

Removing a flag says the opposite: *this will be readable by the AI*. Nothing
changes until you save; saving is what makes it eligible.

<Callout type="warning">
  A copy already released to the client cannot be recalled by flagging the
  document. Revoke that release separately — and even revoking cannot recall
  what has already been read.
</Callout>

## Where you see them

- **Flags** column and the sensitivity filters on the document list.
- **Handle with care** on the document detail sheet, spelling out what the flag
  is currently doing.
- **Withheld** in the analysis [coverage panel](/analysis/coverage), with the
  reason.
- The bulk editor, where each flag is a three-way control: leave, set, or clear.
