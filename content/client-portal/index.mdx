# The client portal

## Client access is a record, not a permission

The portal reads the list of things **released to it** and nothing else. It
never touches a case, a document list or an analysis run, so there is no flag to
misconfigure.

<Callout type="info">
  An empty released list means an empty portal. That is the whole rule.
</Callout>

A client never appears in a firm-wide user list, a search result or a team
picker. They are counted separately from members for exactly that reason.

## What a client sees

**Shared with you** — the documents released on their matter, each with when it
was shared, its page count, and whether it is downloadable or view-only, and
whether it is watermarked.

**Their client channel**, if the lead has opened one. See
[Messaging](/messaging).

Nothing else on the matter is shown, and the portal says so.

## Inviting a client

**Client tab → Invite the client**.

- Enter their email address.
- Invite a delegate the same way. Each person gets their own account — never a
  shared login.

The invitation **names your firm and never the matter**. Access has no end date;
it ends when the matter closes.

Until somebody accepts, the account shows as *invited … awaiting acceptance*.

<Callout type="warning">
  The client portal is a plan capability. On a plan without it, inviting clients
  and releasing documents to them is unavailable — see [Plans and
  limits](/reference/limits).
</Callout>

## Who can do this

Inviting the client and releasing to them belong to the **lead attorney** on
that matter. Everyone else sees the ledger and is told that plainly.

## The released ledger

The **Client** tab lists everything released, live and revoked: what it was, who
released it, when, during which phase, whether the client was notified, and
whether download and watermarking are on.

Four kinds of thing can appear there: a **document**, a **work artifact**, a
**case update**, and a **client thread**.
