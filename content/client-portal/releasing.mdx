# Releasing and revoking

## Releasing a document

**Release to the client** on a document, from the Documents tab. One document at
a time — there is no bulk release.

The dialog asks for two decisions:

**Allow download.** Off means view-only, which is a real share — the client can
read it, they just cannot take a copy.

**Watermark with their name and the date.** Applied to what they open and to
what they download.

## What it does

The document appears in the client's portal. The client is notified and can open
it from that moment. Nothing else on the matter is shared by this.

<Callout type="warning">
  This leaves the firm. Revoking later stops future access — it cannot recall
  what has already been read.
</Callout>

## What cannot be released

Work product and privileged documents never leave the firm. The release button
refuses, and says so.

A document that is not on this matter cannot be released from it either.

## Revoking

**Revoke** on the released ledger, on the Client tab. A **reason** is required.

Future access stops immediately, in every session. Anything the client has
already opened or downloaded stays with them.

Revoked entries stay on the ledger, marked **Revoked**, with who revoked them
and when.

## Release and withdrawal interact

A document that has been released **cannot be withdrawn** from the matter.
Revoke the release first.

Flagging a released document as privileged does not recall it either. Revoke it
separately.

## Phase changes release nothing

Advancing a matter's phase re-evaluates what the client is allowed to see and
**releases nothing**. That stays one deliberate click by the lead. Reverting a
phase never un-shares what was already shared.
