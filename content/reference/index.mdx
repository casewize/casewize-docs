# Glossary

**Alert** — a development the analysis judged urgent enough to surface on its
own. New, acknowledged, dismissed or resolved.

**Arrival** — a record that the same file reached the firm again, from another
custodian or by another route. Several arrivals, one stored object.

**Case access** — the computed answer to whether you can open a matter, and what
you can do on it. Recomputed whenever a grant, wall or role changes.

**Case file** — everything filed on a matter.

**Client account** — a portal-only account belonging to a matter. Reads what has
been released and never the case record.

**Coverage** — what an analysis run was allowed to read, and what was withheld
from it and why.

**Development** — something the analysis judged material since the last run.

**Disclosure / release** — the record that a specific document was made
available to the client. The portal reads these rows and nothing else.

**Draft** — an unfinished intake. Visible only to its creator. A lead, not a
case.

**Ethical wall** — a deny that removes a matter from one person entirely. Beats
every role, including the firm owner's.

**Evidence gap** — something the analysis expected on the file and could not
find, with a suggested way to get it.

**Evidence list** — the indexed set of facts a run's stages cite into. A citation
resolves back to the fact, the document and the page.

**External grant** — access for someone outside the firm to **named documents on
one matter**, with a mandatory expiry.

**Fact** — a proposition an attorney recorded. Handed to every later run; a
locked one is treated as settled.

**Firm role** — the ceiling on what you are capable of. Eight of them.

**Incremental run** — a run that reuses the stages nothing changed under.

**Lead attorney** — exactly one per matter. The only person who may release
anything to the client.

**Legal hold** — a freeze on deletion of a matter and everything under it. Set by
an attorney or the owner; lifted only by the owner.

**Materiality** — how much a development matters: critical, high, moderate, low,
informational.

**Matter** — a case. The unit of work.

**Metadata-only access** — you can see that a matter exists and manage its
roster; you cannot read the file. The firm-administrator position.

**Party** — an entity the firm knows, attached to matters in roles. It exists
once and carries its history across matters.

**Phase** — where the litigation stands. Investigation, discovery, expert
reports, mediation, trial.

**Retention** — how long after closing a matter is kept. Outranked by a legal
hold.

**Run** — one execution of the analysis. Produces a numbered version.

**Stage** — one step of a run. Nine of them, each writing its own row.

**Step-up** — being asked for your password again at the moment of an
irreversible act.

**Strength** — the score out of 100 the analysis puts on a matter, overall and in
nine other categories.

**Version** — a numbered analysis result. Never overwritten.

**Withdrawn** — taken off a matter without being destroyed. Restorable.

**Work product** — the firm's own analysis. Never retrieved by the AI, never
released.
