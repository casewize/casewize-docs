# Plans and limits

| | Free trial | Professional | Firm | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Active matters | 1 | 25 | 100 | Negotiated |
| People (seats) | 5 | 5 | 20 | Negotiated |
| Documents per matter | 5 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Negotiated |
| Largest single file | 10 MB | 250 MB | 500 MB | Negotiated |
| Document storage | 100 MB | 250 GB | 1 TB | Negotiated |
| Analyses per period | 2 | 500 | 2,500 | Negotiated |
| Tasks per matter | 5 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Negotiated |
| Client portal | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Messaging | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

The **free trial** runs for 30 days. It exists to prove the product on a single
matter, not to run a practice, which is why every cap is deliberately small.

**Enterprise** is negotiated per firm, with onboarding, review sessions and a
named point of contact.

## How the meters are counted

**Active matters** — counted across *active* and *on hold*. Drafts are free, and
closed matters do not count.

**Seats** — firm members **plus pending invitations**. An invitation nobody has
accepted still holds a seat. Clients and externals are not seats.

**Analyses per period** — runs that actually called a model, within the current
billing period. A run that carried everything forward does not count.

**Document storage** — across the whole firm.

## What happens at a limit

The refusal is specific: which limit, what your plan covers, and what your firm
has used. Nothing already in the product is deleted, hidden or degraded.

## Plan-locked

When a trial ends or a subscription stops, the firm goes **plan-locked**:

<Callout type="info">
  Every matter, document and analysis stays readable and downloadable. New work
  is paused until a plan is chosen.
</Callout>

## Past due

A failed payment does **not** pause anything while it is retried. Full access
continues; update the card to avoid interruption.

## Where a limit bites

You will meet these as refusals in ordinary flows: opening a matter (active
matters), inviting a colleague (seats), uploading (documents per matter, file
size, storage), running an analysis (analyses per period), and adding a task
(tasks per matter).
