# Billing and plans

**Firm Settings → Billing** shows the current plan, its status, usage against
each meter, and every tier including the one you are on.

Firm owners and the billing role can manage it.

## Status

| Status | |
| --- | --- |
| **Trial** | A new firm's first 30 days. |
| **Active** | |
| **Payment failed** | We could not take payment. Full access continues while we retry — update the card. |
| **Paused** | |
| **Cancelled** | The plan ends at the close of the current period. |

## Usage meters

Active matters · People · Documents stored · Analyses this period.

Active matters counts both **active** and **on hold**. Drafts are free. Seats
count members **and pending invitations** — an invitation nobody has accepted
still holds a seat.

## When a limit is hit

The product refuses the new thing and tells you which limit and by how much. It
never deletes, hides or degrades what is already there.

<Callout type="info">
  **New work is paused** — the trial ended, or the subscription is not active.
  Every matter, document and analysis stays readable and downloadable. Choose a
  plan to add new work again.
</Callout>

Typical refusals:

- Your plan covers *n* active matters and your firm has *m*.
- Every seat on your plan is taken, counting invitations nobody has accepted yet.
- Your plan allows *n* documents per matter and this one already has *m*.
- That file is over the single-file limit on your plan.
- Your firm has used *x* of its *y* of document storage.
- Your plan includes *n* analyses per billing period and your firm has used *m*.
- The client portal / messaging is not included in your firm's plan.

## Changing plan

Pick a tier to compare it — nothing is charged until you continue.

- Moving onto a paid plan changes it as soon as the card is saved. **A trial
  still runs to its end date before anything is charged.**
- Moving between paid plans is handled by Stripe, including the proration and
  the invoice for the difference.
- **Enterprise** is negotiated. Contact sales.
- The trial is only available when a firm first signs up.

**Manage billing** opens Stripe for plan changes, invoices, receipts and
cancellation.
