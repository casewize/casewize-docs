# The firm

**Firm Settings** carries the rules that apply to every matter. Your own
credentials and preferences live on [Your account](/account).

Firm owners and firm administrators can open it. Everyone else is told the
screen exists and cannot open it: *your role does not include changing firm-wide
rules or the roster*.

## Firm identity

**Firm name** — how the firm reads everywhere.

**Main jurisdiction**

**Matter types** — every kind of matter the firm takes. These lead the list on a
new case, above every other type. At least one must stay selected.

**Retention (years)** — how long after closing a matter stays before it becomes
eligible for deletion. The clock starts at close.

<Callout type="warning">
  A legal hold outranks retention. A held matter is never deleted, whatever this
  setting says.
</Callout>

**Record** — the workspace slug and the date the firm was opened. Read-only.

## Opening another firm

**Open a new firm** in the workspace switcher creates a separate firm with its
own cases, members and retention. Nothing is shared with the firms you are
already in, and you become its owner.

You will not be able to approve AI output or release anything until a bar number
is recorded on your account.
