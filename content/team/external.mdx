# Outside experts and co-counsel

An outsider is not a member with fewer permissions. They are granted **named
documents on one matter**, with an expiry date.

## Granting access

**Add to the matter → An outsider**.

- Pick someone who already holds an outside seat at the firm, or **invite a new
  one by email** — the seat and the grant are created together when they accept.
- Tick the documents the grant reaches. A grant is to named documents, never to
  the matter. At least one is required.
- Set an **expiry date**. The field will not take a blank.

Work product and privileged documents are never in that list, whatever anyone
ticks.

## What no grant here reaches

Regardless of which documents you name, an external grant never reaches:

- Work product
- Risk and settlement figures
- Attorney notes
- Client communications
- Fees and billing
- Unreviewed AI output
- Any other matter

## The expiry

<Callout type="warning">
  Access dies on the date whether or not anyone remembers. The lead is reminded
  seven days out — that reminder is a courtesy; the date is the control.
</Callout>

**Revoke this grant** ends it early.

## What the outsider sees

They do not get the product. They get **Shared with you**: the matters they have
been given documents on, the documents themselves, and when the access ends.

The page says so plainly — *you see only what was shared, and only until the
access ends* — and each matter carries a *named documents only* badge.

## Across the firm

**Firm Settings → People** has an *External access, across every matter* panel
listing every outstanding grant, how many documents each reaches, and which are
expiring. Grants are created from the matter, never from there.
