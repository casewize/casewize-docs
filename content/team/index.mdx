# The case team

The **Team** tab is the access list for one matter: firm members on it, external
grants, client accounts, ethical walls — and, deliberately, everyone at the firm
who is *not* on it.

## Firm members on this matter

A firm role sets the ceiling. The assignment decides whether it applies here at
all. There is no separate role to pick when you add someone: their firm role
decides what they can do once inside.

Each person on the roster shows what their assignment actually carries:

- Reads the file
- Writes to the file
- Approves AI output
- Releases to the client
- Manages the roster

## Adding a colleague

**Add to the matter → A colleague**. Pick the person, optionally record why
(*second-chairing the depositions*), and add them.

The conflict check runs against the firm's parties before anyone is added, and
the lead attorney is notified either way — **including when a firm owner or
administrator adds themselves**. That notice is what stops "manages access" from
quietly becoming "has access".

If nobody is left to add, everyone eligible is already on the matter or walled
off from it.

## Removing someone

**Remove from the matter**. Access ends on their **next request**, not at their
next sign-in.

<Callout type="info">
  Removing someone does not remove what they did. Their name stays on every
  entry, approval and audit line they created, with the role they held at the
  time.
</Callout>

## Not on this matter

The interesting half of an access list. It counts the firm members with **no
access row** at all — for whom this matter does not appear in any list, count or
search.

Administering the firm is not the same as being on a matter, and this panel is
where that becomes visible.

An ethical wall is a different thing again: a deny that beats every role, lifted
by a recorded waiver rather than by adding somebody back. See [Ethical
walls](/team/walls).
