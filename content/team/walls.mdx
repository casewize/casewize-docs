# Ethical walls

A wall removes a matter from someone **entirely**, rather than marking it as
off-limits.

## What it does

<Callout type="warning">
  The walled person sees **no trace** of the matter — not in lists, search,
  notifications, AI answers or counts. It beats every role, including the firm
  owner's.
</Callout>

It is evaluated before anything else, which means a walled person fails every
check identically to someone who was simply never assigned. There is no locked
row, no greyed-out entry, nothing to notice.

## Wall versus absence

They are not the same.

- **Absence from the roster** means no access row. The matter does not appear
  for that person.
- **A wall** is an active deny. It survives being added to the matter, and it
  beats a firm role that would otherwise reach the file.

That is why the Team tab lists them separately, and why lifting a wall is not
the same as adding somebody back.

## Setting one

**Wall someone off** on the Team tab. Pick the person and type a **reason —
kept permanently**: why must this person not see the matter?

Attorneys and the firm owner can set a wall.

## Lifting one

**Lift the wall**, with a reason kept permanently. It is recorded as a
**waiver, never a deletion** — the fact that a wall existed survives, with who
set it, who lifted it, and both reasons.

## Where walls are visible

- The matter's **Team** tab, active and lifted.
- **Firm Settings → People**, across every matter, as *person — matter*.

They are not listed on your own account. A walled matter shows no trace
anywhere, and a line saying you are walled off one would be the disclosure the
wall exists to prevent.

## Walls and the conflict check

A conflict check runs as the system, so it sees walled matters. It reports that
something matched and who it matched — and nothing else. The matter is not
named, and nobody on it is named.
