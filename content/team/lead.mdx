# The lead attorney

Exactly one lead at all times.

## What the lead alone can do

**Release to the client.** Not any attorney on the matter, and not the firm
owner unless they hold the lead. Releasing and revoking on a matter belong to
its lead alone.

**Open a client thread to the client.** A client channel is created shut; the
lead opens it.

The lead is also who gets notified when someone is added to the matter, and who
is reminded seven days before an external grant expires.

## Who can be lead

Only members who hold a **bar number** on file. The same licence is what grants
the right to approve AI output.

If you are not in the list, record your bar number and the state that issued it
on [Your account](/account).

## Transferring the lead

**Transfer the lead** on the Team tab. Pick the new lead, then decide what
happens to the outgoing one:

- **Stays on the matter** — keeps their assignment and everything it carries,
  minus the release right.
- **Comes off the matter** — access ends on their next request, not at their next
  sign-in.

<Callout type="info">
  The transfer is atomic: never zero leads, never two. The Settings tab shows
  the lead but will not change it — handing a matter over happens on the Team
  tab, by the outgoing lead or the firm owner.
</Callout>

If nobody else on the matter holds a bar number, add an attorney to the roster
first.

## A matter cannot be left without one

Two rules follow from that:

- You cannot remove the lead from a matter. Transfer it first.
- A firm member who still leads at least one matter cannot be deactivated. *A
  case cannot be left without a lead attorney, even for an afternoon.*

## No lead

A matter with no lead — possible only in transitional states — cannot release
anything to the client and says so.
