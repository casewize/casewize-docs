# How CaseWize works

## The firm is the container

Everything belongs to a firm: matters, documents, people, retention, billing.
You can hold a seat at more than one firm — the workspace switcher at the top of
the sidebar moves between them, and nothing is shared across the boundary.

Opening a new firm makes you its owner. Until a bar number is recorded on your
account you cannot approve AI output or release anything, whatever your role
says.

## A matter is the unit of work

A matter — the product also calls it a case — carries an internal number unique
to your firm, a court number once one exists, a matter type, a jurisdiction, a
phase, and two fields it cannot go active without: a **statute deadline** and a
**lead attorney**.

Inside a matter, work is split across tabs:

| Tab | What lives there |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Strength, open alerts, next actions, recent developments |
| Parties | Who is involved, and the conflict check they trigger |
| Documents | The case file — everything filed on the matter |
| Analysis | Scores, developments, gaps, contradictions, actions, the memo |
| Chronology | Every dated event the documents produced, in order |
| Tasks | The board for this matter |
| Messages | Case-team threads and the client channel |
| Team | Who is on the matter, outside grants, ethical walls |
| Client | What has been released, and the client's accounts |
| Settings | The matter's own fields, status and retention |

## Two planes of authority

Your **firm role** decides what you are capable of. Your **case assignment**
decides whether a matter exists for you at all. The effective answer is the
intersection, minus any ethical wall — and a wall always wins.

A matter you are not on is not hidden from you in a list. It is absent from the
list, the counts and the search alike.

<Callout type="info">
  A firm administrator is the clearest illustration: they run the roster and the
  billing, so they can see that a matter exists and manage who is on it, and
  they cannot open the file. That is deliberate, and no setting changes it.
</Callout>

## The analysis reads the file, not your notes

When a document is filed it is read in the background — the text is extracted
with its page numbers intact, so anything the analysis says can be traced to a
page. An analysis run then works through the file in stages and writes a new
version: a case summary, the legal research, a theory and damages model, a set
of scores, a memo, and the developments since last time.

Privileged and work-product documents are excluded from all of it. So are
documents that have been withdrawn, and documents recorded on the matter with no
file behind them.

## Nothing the engine writes is final

Every finding can be approved, rejected, marked incorrect or suppressed. Scores
can be overridden with a reason. Chronology rows can be corrected, struck or
frozen, and a row you touch stops being the engine's. Facts you record are
handed to every later run.
